Our Mission

EZIOKWU AI aims to make information about artificial intelligence systems and robots more accessible, understandable and transparent through independent research and evidence-based evaluation.

Safety Classification

A preliminary classification framework designed to communicate assessed risk and verification status. Every assessment must be supported by documented evidence.

GREEN — Lower assessed risk

Lower risk within the specific scope and conditions assessed.

YELLOW — Moderate risk

Potential concerns identified. Caution and monitoring recommended.

ORANGE — Elevated risk

Significant concerns requiring enhanced safeguards and controls.

RED — High assessed risk

Serious hazards or unacceptable risks identified within the assessed scope.

GREY — Unverified

Insufficient evidence to support an independent assessment.

Important: These categories are a proposed assessment framework, not an official certification or guarantee of safety. A classification applies only to the documented assessment scope.

Our Methodology

EZIOKWU AI intends to examine system identity, intended use, performance, reliability, autonomy, safety controls, cybersecurity, human oversight and documented incidents. Each published assessment should identify its scope, test conditions, supporting evidence, limitations and date.

01 — Identity

Identification of the AI system, manufacturer, version and declared purpose.

02 — Performance

Examination of capabilities, reliability and documented limitations.

03 — Safety

Review of safeguards, human oversight, risks and potential hazards.

04 — Evidence

Documentation of sources, assessment conditions, findings and limitations.
