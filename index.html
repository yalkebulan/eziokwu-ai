Our Mission

We aim to make information about AI systems and robots accessible, understandable and transparent for everyone.

Safety Classification

Five risk levels and one verification status. Each assessment must be supported by documented evidence.

GREEN

Low risk within assessed scope.

YELLOW

Moderate risk. Caution recommended.

ORANGE

Elevated risk. Enhanced controls.

RED

High risk. Serious hazards identified.

GREY

Unverified. Evidence insufficient.

Our Methodology

AI TRUST intends to examine identity, intended use, safety controls, performance, reliability, autonomy, cybersecurity and documented incidents. Results will include scope, test conditions, evidence, limitations and assessment date.
